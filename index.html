Privacy Policy

UTOPYA BV · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how UTOPYA BV ("we", "us") processes personal data of customers who buy our products through online marketplaces such as Temu. We process personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR.

1. Controller and contact

UTOPYA BV, registered in the Netherlands.
Email for privacy questions and requests: utopyabv@gmail.com

2. What data we process

When you place an order with us through a marketplace, the marketplace shares with us only the data needed to deliver your order:

  • Name
  • Delivery address
  • Phone number and email address, where provided by the marketplace for delivery and customer-service purposes
  • Order information (order number, products, SKU, quantity, shipping and tracking details)

We do not receive or store your payment details. Payments are handled by the marketplace.

3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

PurposeLegal basis
Packing and shipping your order, printing shipping labels, tracking deliveryPerformance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Handling returns, cancellations and customer servicePerformance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Keeping accounting and tax recordsLegal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR)

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling or automated decision-making, and we never sell it.

4. Who we share it with

  • Carriers (for example Royal Mail) – to deliver your parcel.
  • Our fulfilment warehouse – to pick, pack and ship your order.
  • IT service providers acting as processors under a data processing agreement, for example cloud hosting (Render, servers in Frankfurt, Germany) and file storage (Google).
  • The marketplace you ordered from (for example Temu), which provides the order data to us.

5. Where your data is stored

Our order-processing systems run on servers located in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Data is transmitted using encrypted connections (TLS 1.2 or higher). If any data is transferred outside the EEA or UK, we use appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

6. How long we keep it

  • Shipping labels and delivery data in our order-processing systems: deleted within 30 days after the order is shipped.
  • Invoices and accounting records: kept for 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.

7. How we protect it

Access to personal data is limited to authorised persons who need it to fulfil orders, protected by authentication and logged. Stored files are encrypted at rest by our cloud providers. If a personal data breach occurs, we notify the competent supervisory authority within 72 hours where required, and inform the affected marketplace and individuals when necessary.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To use these rights, email utopyabv@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or the authority in your country.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always published on this page with its update date.
